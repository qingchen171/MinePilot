## Provenance and evidence classification

- **Production:** shipped catalog has only `level-001` (9×9, 10 Mines, no obstacles). Fresh-install flows are identified separately from deterministic preloaded L1 snapshots. The latter use the real production catalog generator, strict Save v4 mapping/validation, guarded session and real browser, but are **not** fresh-install promises.
- **Controlled catalog:** the existing L2/L3/L5 and multi-Reward tests use a test-only validated catalog shared by projection and execution. They do not add shipped Levels.
- **Validated compatibility fixture:** one-time/L4 and saved Benben entitlement paths are strict preloaded Save v4 states. They prove accepted-state compatibility, **not** current production Reward generation or one continuous natural Benben history.
- **Actual fault injection** means a test changes storage, lease, head, or writer conditions before a real operation. **Behavior-only** means an observed committed/rejected/reloaded state without such an injected failure. Neither label implies a mutation operator was injected into production source.

## Fault-injection and defect ledger

Actual new injections: G2 inactive-slot storage failures on Purchase and Start, head-write uncertainty on Leave, stale revision and runId rejection; G4 inactive-slot failure on third Failure. Existing actual injections: competing writer/lease and stale authority in S5-03/S5-04/S5-05/S5-07/S5-08 integration and browser suites, including retained Retry and Recovery. All other route, UI, F5 and layout assertions are behavior-only; no production-source mutation operators were injected in S5-09.

At implementation evidence authoring, no production defect was confirmed. The first draft of the G8 browser assertion compared a post-scroll cell to a pre-scroll canvas rectangle and tried to inspect focus without first entering keyboard navigation; those were test-measurement errors corrected before acceptance. The normal build's large-chunk notice is an existing informational performance warning, not a claimed P0/P1 defect or Stage 6 optimization authorization. The final Reviewer and Elio must still record any later P0/P1 and P2 dispositions before freeze.
